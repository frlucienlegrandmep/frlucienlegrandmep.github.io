## About the Project

**Footsteps in Antiquity** is an interactive digital flipbook archive commemorating the 100th birthday of priest and biblical scholar **Prof. Dr. Lucien Legrand, MEP** of St. Peter’s Pontifical Seminary & Institute, Bengaluru.

Preserving original archival photographs and reflections from his pilgrimages to the Holy Land in 1950–1952, this digital edition brings two commemorative volumes to life in a tactile, browser-based reading experience:

* **Volume 1 — Footsteps in Antiquity: Jerusalem** *(165 illustrated archival folios)*
* **Volume 2 — Footsteps in Antiquity: Trans-Jordan**

---

## Key Features

* **Realistic Page-Turning Physics:** Drag or click any page corner—or use keyboard arrows and on-screen controls—to flip through pages like a physical archival volume.
* **Single Page & Spread Views:** Switch seamlessly between a large single-page A4 view for detailed reading and a classic two-page open-book spread.
* **Optical Magnifier & Zoom:** Inspect fine archival details and captions using the interactive 2.25× optical magnifier lens and precision zoom controls.
* **Distraction-Free Fullscreen Mode:** Immersive dark-espresso theater mode engineered to fit cleanly within any viewport.
* **Quick-Browse Scrubber:** Jump effortlessly to any folio across both volumes using the interactive page slider.
* **Optimized Archival Imagery:** High-resolution digitized folios served in lightweight WebP format for fast loading on desktop and mobile devices.
